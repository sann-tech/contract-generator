1Information We Collect

DevZan collects only the minimum information necessary to provide the Service:

Data TypeSourcePurpose
Name & EmailGoogle Sign-InAuthentication & auto-fill
Profile PhotoGoogle Sign-InDisplay in sidebar
Contract DataYou (entered manually)Contract generation & storage
SMTP CredentialsYou (entered manually)Sending emails — not stored server-side

We do not collect payment information, browsing history, or any data beyond what is needed to operate DevZan.

2How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity via Google OAuth
  • Pre-fill your developer details in contracts
  • Save and sync your contracts across devices (signed-in users only)
  • Display your name and avatar in the app interface

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3Data Storage

Guest users: All contract data is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. DevZan's servers do not store any of your data.

Signed-in users: Contracts are stored in DevZan's database hosted on Render. Your Google profile info (name, email, avatar) is stored to maintain your session.

SMTP passwords you enter are used only to send emails in the moment and are never stored on our servers.

4Third-Party Services

DevZan uses the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth — for sign-in authentication. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Render — for hosting the application and database.
  • Google Fonts — for typography. May log font requests.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

5Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated contracts
  • Export your contract data at any time
  • Revoke Google OAuth access via your Google Account settings

To request account deletion, contact us using the details in Section 9.

6Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

You are responsible for keeping your Google account credentials secure.

7Children's Privacy

DevZan is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of DevZan after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9Contact

For privacy concerns, data deletion requests, or any questions about this policy, please contact the DevZan developer directly through the app or via the email address listed in your generated contracts.
